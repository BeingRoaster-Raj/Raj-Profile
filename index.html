Probso

Upload a PDF and get back an MP4 lecture: every page becomes a Manim animation with a teacher-style voice-over.

  • A multimodal LLM writes the narration, Piper TTS speaks it, and the timings drive the animation

  • Parallel rendering, ffmpeg muxing and Cloudinary delivery
